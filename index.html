I am a PhD student at INSA Rennes (IRISA, DiverSE team) and Nokia Bell Labs, on a CIFRE contract that started in February 2026. My advisors are Mathieu Acher and Dominique Verchere.

Asked a causal question, a language model will often match on the wording. Asked to write down the causal graph first and answer from it, it gets more answers right, and we can check whether the answer really used the graph. That was my first PhD paper. The rest of the thesis applies the same idea to networks, where the structure is a service model with hard constraints.

Before this I did an MSc at École Polytechnique and a BEng at South China University of Technology, and spent six months on robot navigation at the Institute of Automation of the Chinese Academy of Sciences. I also like building data systems end to end; four of them are above.
